Supervised and unsupervised learning, developed from first principles through diagnostics, reproducible Python examples, and solved problems.

This independent study edition follows Prof. Sayantee Jana’s IIT Hyderabad course. Its core is the geometry and statistical meaning of a model: what is fitted, which assumptions support an interpretation, and what evidence can reveal a failure.

Begin with variation and multivariate structure, then develop regression, diagnosis, model assessment, classification, and a local causal design. Familiarity with linear algebra, calculus, and elementary probability is assumed. Subject-specific definitions and derivations appear where they are needed.
